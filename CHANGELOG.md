# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Registry: a machine-readable `call` per area, and the connection checks read only the areas that should be connected (2026-10-30).** `hubs.csv` gains `call` (adopt, coexist, keep, watch or avoid; blank counts as coexist), filled from each recommendation. The checks that need a consumer to have connected an area (by configuration, one seam, selectable, a fake hub) are cases only for areas whose call is adopt or coexist, and the scorer's `seam_share` and `selectable_share` are over those areas (`to_connect`, with the unbound ones listed as `unconnected`). Weights and thresholds are unchanged. A consumer is no longer behind on an area the research says to keep, watch or avoid.
- **Scholarly search: an OpenAlex arm of the `know-the-world` seat (2026-10-30; built offline, not selected, not measured).** `hubofhubs.science.openalex.OpenAlexWorks` (`@implementation("know-the-world", "openalex")`, extra `science`) searches works with year and open-access filters and returns title, authors, year, venue, DOI, citations, an abstract rebuilt from the inverted index and a landing link, with a typed error per failure, a cache, spacing and a daily cap. `hubofhubs.science.crossref.lookup_doi` checks a DOI. Port values in `hubofhubs.ports.scholarly`. Key and contact address are arguments. See `docs/science-search.md`.
- **Scholarly search review fixes (2026-10-30).** The result states that titles and abstracts are untrusted third-party text and that only abstracts were read; titles, authors and abstracts are one line each; a landing page must be http or https; searches leave retracted works out (`is_retracted:false`, `include_retracted` to keep them) and a DOI lookup marks one RETRACTED; the cache holds at most `cache_size` (256) entries, least recently used out; `limiter=` lets a consumer keep one daily count across rebuilt arms; new `hubofhubs.science.citations` compares DOIs in a reply with the DOIs a turn held.
